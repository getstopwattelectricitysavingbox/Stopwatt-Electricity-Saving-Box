The Science Behind Power Factor Correction
StopWatt's primary technical claim rests on power factor correction—a real concept in electrical engineering. But understanding where it applies reveals why the device cannot do what it promises in a residential setting.
What Power Factor Actually Is
The Definition
Power factor is the ratio of real power (useful work performed) to apparent power (total power supplied). A lower ratio means more current is drawn to accomplish the same output—genuinely wasteful in industrial contexts.
